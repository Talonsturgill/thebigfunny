"A software error may prevent the vehicle from making the pedestrian warning sound at certain speeds."
"Dealers will replace the digital signal processing module in Nautilus Hybrid vehicles equipped with 28 speakers, free of charge. The remedy is currently under development for all other Nautilus and Explorer Hybrid vehicles affected by this recall."

Not claimed, and why. The record never says the 28 speakers are what failed, and the
external pedestrian alert emitter is generally not part of the cabin audio array, so
this episode places the speaker count and the missing sound beside each other as two
separately true facts and never asserts one caused the other. We also do not subtract
43,438 from 66,383: the two campaigns cover different model year ranges, so the
difference is not a count of newly added vehicles. Both totals are stated as filed.
