# WHO IS WATCHING

Locked by the owner 2026-08-02: **18 to 34**, TikTok's core band, which is also
where this show's grievances actually live.

This file exists because every other document here describes what the show IS.
None of them described who it is FOR, so every judgement call about register got
made in a vacuum. Read it before writing a line.

## The one-sentence version

They are the people the story happened to. Not an audience being informed about
renters, gig workers and fee payers. **The renters.**

That changes the job from EXPLAINING to NAMING. They do not need to be told that
tenant screening is unfair; they got the denial email. What they have never had
is the document proving it was not their fault.

## What the research actually says, and what it costs us

Two things about this band's humour are well documented and both have teeth:

1. **It is a "performance of authenticity."** Self-mockery and relatability
   beat polish. The audience is fluent in being marketed to and can smell a
   brand voice in about a second.
2. **Dark humour is a language of SOLIDARITY**, a way of saying "I have been
   there too" without getting earnest or heavy-handed. Gen Z tends to put the
   pain in the open and make the pain itself the joke; the millennial half of
   the band reaches for sarcasm, nihilism and absurdism as distance. Our band
   spans both.

The lucky part: the cast already serves both halves. **Ray is the pain named out
loud. Dee is the absurdist distance.** That is why the two-hander works and why
neither of them should drift toward the other's register.

The expensive part, and the rule that follows from it:

> ### NO SLANG. EVER.
>
> The owner asked about "the lingo". The honest answer from the research is that
> reaching for the lingo is the single fastest way to lose this audience, because
> a brand performing youth is the exact opposite of the authenticity they respond
> to. It also ROTS: a 2026 phrase is embarrassing by 2027, and this show is
> building a numbered archive people can scroll back through.
>
> Talk like a PEER, not like a brand doing youth outreach. That means
> contractions, short sentences, plain spoken English, and zero journalese. It
> does not mean "no cap" or whatever is current the week you read this.
>
> The one thing that never dates is a real document and a person reacting to it
> the way you would.

## What makes them laugh, in order

1. **The absurd true specific.** Not "screening companies are careless" but
   "it counted the same eviction twice". The fact does the work; this is the
   whole thesis of the show and it happens to be exactly right for this band.
2. **Deadpan understatement on something insane.** Dee's register. The gap
   between how enormous the thing is and how flat it is said IS the joke.
3. **The thing everybody noticed and nobody said.** Recognition comedy. When it
   lands the comment is "why is this so accurate", which is a share.
4. **Being on their side against a thing, never above them about it.**

## What loses them, in order

1. **EARNESTNESS.** The research is blunt: solidarity works only when it stays
   un-earnest. A closing line that states the moral is a lecture, and a lecture
   from a cartoon is worse than a lecture.
2. **Explaining the joke.** Already the house rule. This audience is faster than
   the script and finishing the thought for them reads as condescension.
3. **Sounding like the news.** "Officials say", "critics argue", "sparked
   outrage". Instant channel change.
4. **Slang, memes, or a trend reference used as a bid for relevance.**
5. **Both-sidesing it.** Not because they want partisanship, but because a
   hedge reads as being scared of the story. The villain is an institution, and
   naming it plainly is the point.

## Three things about the FORMAT this band decides on

- **The first second is the whole audition.** Not the first three. The hook is
  the fact, flat, with no preamble and no logo.
- **It is watched MUTED.** The burned-in caption is not an accessibility
  feature, it is the script. If the joke needs the audio it does not exist.
- **Receipts are respected.** Sources in the first comment reads as confidence.
  It is also the single strongest defence the account has.

## The test to apply to a finished script

Not "is this accurate" and not "is this outrageous". Ask:

> **Would a 26 year old send this to the group chat with no comment?**

No comment is the bar. If it needs the sender to add "this is insane", the
script did not land it.
